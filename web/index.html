What we do

Repairs

Walk in, no booking needed. A flat tyre repair takes about 30 minutes and costs 15 euros. A full service takes one day and costs 79 euros.

Rentals

We rent city bikes for 20 euros a day and e-bikes for 40 euros a day. Bring an ID card.

Opening hours

We are open Monday to Saturday, from 9 am to 6 pm. We are closed on Sunday and on public holidays.

Returns

You can return a bike within 30 days if it is unused, with the receipt. Repairs cannot be returned.

Contact

Call us on +1 555 0142 or write to hello@maplebikes.example. Our address is 12 Harbour Street.
